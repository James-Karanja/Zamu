# Glossary — Zamu

- **Bursary** — public grant toward a student's school fees.
- **MCA** — Member of County Assembly; controls a ward bursary fund.
- **MP / NG-CDF** — Member of Parliament; National Government Constituencies Development Fund, which includes constituency bursaries.
- **County bursary** — bursary funded from the county government (governor's) budget.
- **Chief** — local national-government administrator; traditionally announces distributions and posts beneficiary lists.
- **CHV** — Community Health Volunteer; already visits households.
- **HELB** — Higher Education Loans Board; Kenya's digitised, means-tested university loan system.
- **Controller of Budget** — authorises withdrawals of public funds.
- **Auditor General** — audits use of public funds.
- **USSD** — menu-driven session protocol on any GSM phone (e.g. `*384#`); no internet, no free-text messaging.
- **Turn (Zamu)** — a child's position in the round's ranked queue.
- **Round** — one bursary funding window, from opening to school confirmation.
